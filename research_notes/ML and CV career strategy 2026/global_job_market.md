# Global (US/Europe) Job Market for ML, DL and CV Roles, 2025–2026 (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 17 Oct 2026. About 17 tool calls (searches and fetches). Primary sources fetched directly: SignalFire State of Talent 2025, Levels.fyi 2025 annual report, Lightcast's AI Index 2026 summary, Indeed Hiring Lab (Jan 2026 and Sep 2026). Most other figures come from search-result summaries or secondary aggregators and are marked as such. Evidence is tagged **[DATA]** (measured), **[SURVEY/PROJECTION]** (employer survey or forecast), **[SECONDARY]** (an aggregator citing an original I did not open) or **[OPINION/PRESS]**.

## 1. What the major indices say about AI/ML demand in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
All the big trackers show AI-skill demand growing fast. AI-skill postings rose about +55% YoY in 2025 (Lightcast/AI Index 2026), and AI-mentioning postings rose about +130% in 2025 (Indeed). Overall tech hiring meanwhile stays well below 2020: tech postings were about 34% below Feb 2020 at the end of 2025. The market is splitting in two, with AI/ML roles growing inside a shrunken tech hiring base. Headline percentages differ a lot by method: Lightcast puts AI at 2.5–2.6% of postings, Indeed at 4.2–6%+, FrogHire at about 19%.

### Cited Findings
**Stanford AI Index 2026 (Lightcast data, released Apr 2026, covers 2025)**
- [DATA] 2.5% of all US job postings mentioned AI skills in 2025. That is +55% vs 2024, +72% vs 2022 and about +297% vs a decade earlier. — [Lightcast, Four Takeaways from the 2026 AI Index (13 Apr 2026)](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/stanford-ai-2026)
- [DATA] The "Artificial Intelligence" skill cluster nearly doubled in a year. "Agentic AI" skills went from 0.06% of postings (2024) to 0.23% (2025), +280%, or about 90,000 US postings. — [Lightcast](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/stanford-ai-2026)
  - Conflict: Pin.com attributes "+10,854% agentic AI listings in one year" to the AI Index 2026. This is inconsistent with Lightcast's own +280% (it may be a different, narrower title-based measure). Treat it as unverified. — [Pin.com tech job market report](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)
- [DATA] Python was the top specialized skill, in 258,674 US postings (2025), up nearly 30% vs 2024. Lightcast says deployment skills (AWS, scalability, workflow management) were among the fastest long-term growers, but gives no figures. — [Lightcast](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/stanford-ai-2026)
- [DATA] Share of postings requiring AI skills by country, 2025: Singapore 4.77%, Hong Kong 3.5%, Luxembourg 3.4%, Spain 3.3%, US 2.6%. In the US, DC has the highest concentration (4.46%), then Delaware (4.43%). California has the most AI jobs by count, then Texas and New York. — [Lightcast](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/stanford-ai-2026)

- Definition caveat: Lightcast counts a posting as an "AI job" if it lists at least one AI skill, including AI ethics and governance. Online postings only. — [Our World in Data (AI Index 2026 data, updated 20 Apr 2026)](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-artificial-intelligence-job-postings)
- [SECONDARY] The AI Index 2026 reportedly says entry-level software developer employment fell nearly 20% from its 2024 peak. — cited by [Pin.com](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/). **Europe / UK**

Caveat: PwC sells AI services, and a premium jump of this size deserves scrutiny.
- [DATA] EU countries in the AI Index top 5 for AI posting share: Luxembourg 3.4%, Spain 3.3%. — [Lightcast](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/stanford-ai-2026)

### Inferences
- The hard data agree on direction (AI demand up strongly in 2025) but differ 2–8x on level, because of definitions. The report should quote a source-specific figure, never "X% of jobs are AI".
- Indeed's note that traditional AI/ML roles declined after early 2022, combined with the explosion of "AI engineer" on LinkedIn, suggests demand is shifting from classic ML/data-science titles toward applied LLM/"AI engineer" titles and toward AI as a skill required in many ordinary roles.
- Tech overall remains at roughly 60–80% of its 2020 posting level depending on the measure and date. ML postings outperform it, but the pool of total openings is not booming.

### Gaps
- No primary 2026 Indeed figure specifically for ML engineer job titles; the latest is July 2025, secondary.
- I could not open the AI Index 2026 chapter PDF, so its exact ML/CV skill-cluster numbers (e.g., computer vision or NLP posting shares) are not captured. The AI Index historically reports skill-cluster breakdowns including computer vision; check the report.
- No pan-European (EU-wide) AI posting totals found. Lightcast/Indeed country-level data for Germany, France and the Netherlands were not retrieved.

## 2. Demand split across roles (MLE, applied scientist, research, CV/perception, MLOps/infra, AI engineer, robotics, edge)

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced growth is in "AI engineer"/LLM-application roles (LinkedIn #1 for 2025 and 2026) and in agentic-AI skills (+280% share, Lightcast). ML engineer postings also outperform general SWE (Indeed index 159 vs 51, Jul 2025). Indeed reports that traditional data-science/AI-ML roles declined from their 2022 peak. I found no rigorous public posting counts for CV/perception, MLOps or edge AI as distinct categories. Evidence on those is mostly qualitative or comes from career blogs.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA] AI engineer is the #1 fastest-growing US job (LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise 2026). AI/ML researcher also ranks high, as does data annotator, which reflects demand for labeling and data work. — [letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/linkedin-names-fastest-growing-jobs-for-2026-bf17881e)
- [DATA] Agentic AI skill postings went from 0.06% to 0.23% (2024 to 2025). Deployment and production skills (AWS, scalability, workflow management) are among the fastest long-run growers. This supports MLOps/infra/LLM-ops demand. — [Lightcast](https://lightcast.io/resources/blog/stanford-ai-2026)
- [SECONDARY] ML engineer postings at 159 (Feb 2020 = 100) vs software engineer at 51 (Jul 2025). — [Pin.com citing Indeed](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)
- [DATA] "Traditional AI/ML roles in data and computer science declined after peaking in early 2022." — [Indeed Hiring Lab, Jan 2026](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/01/22/january-labor-market-update-jobs-mentioning-ai-are-growing-amid-broader-hiring-weakness/)
- [SECONDARY/vendor] SignalFire's later analysis reportedly shows AI/ML engineers +39% and front-end roles -25%. A secondary source attributes this to a "2026" report, which I could not verify. — [pasqualepillitteri.it summary](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/6543/ai-engineering-jobs-signalfire-data-most-resilient-2025)
- [DATA] SignalFire 2025: more than 65% of AI engineers are in the SF Bay Area plus NYC combined. — [SignalFire State of Talent 2025 (20 May 2025)](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)
- [OPINION/industry blogs] CV hiring is fragmenting into sub-specialties: robotics needs ROS 2 and real-time control, autonomous driving needs LiDAR/sensor fusion, medical imaging needs DICOM and FDA familiarity. "General-purpose ML engineers rarely possess the pixel-level expertise needed for production vision systems". — [People in AI, Hiring CV engineers 2026](https://www.peopleinai.com/blog/hiring-computer-vision-engineers-2026); [KORE1 CV engineer JD 2026](https://www.kore1.com/computer-vision-engineer-job-description-template/)
- [DATA, gap] O*NET has no "computer vision engineer" occupation, so no official BLS projection exists for CV specifically. The closest proxies are software developers and computer & information research scientists. — [KORE1](https://www.kore1.com/computer-vision-engineer-job-description-template/)
- [SECONDARY, unsourced] Claims of more than 35%/yr CV job growth to 2030 come from career blogs with no data provenance. Disregard them as evidence. — [search results incl. propelgrad, research.com](https://propelgrad.com/ai-jobs/computer-vision-engineer)
- [DATA, snapshot] Live Indeed search counts (Oct 2026, not a time series): about 26,000 results for "machine learning engineer", about 20,000 for "AI ML engineer", about 36,000 for "artificial intelligence machine learning", about 3,000 for "generative AI LLM". These are raw keyword hits and not deduplicated. — [Indeed MLE search](https://www.indeed.com/q-machine-learning-engineer-jobs.html); [Indeed AI ML](https://www.indeed.com/q-artificial-intelligence-machine-learning-jobs.html)
- [DATA] Robotics: Tesla posted about 110 open Optimus roles in late 2025 (AI, mechanical, manufacturing controls, actuators; Austin and Fremont). — [RoboHorizon (Dec 2025)](https://robohorizon.com/en-us/news/2025/12/tesla-optimus-hiring-spree-hits-110-jobs/); [Teslarati (Mar 2026)](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-optimus-job-listings/)

### Inferences
- Roughly from strongest to weakest evidence: AI engineer / LLM app (growing strongly), ML infra/deployment/agentic (growing), ML engineer (above SWE baseline), classic data scientist / "AI/ML" titles (flat-to-down from 2022 peak), CV/perception, robotics and edge (plausibly growing in robotics/AV/defense pockets, but no quantitative series found).
- CV is better seen as a specialization *inside* ML engineer, robotics and AV postings than as a standalone title. Posting-title counts will therefore undercount CV demand.

### Gaps
- No quantitative posting series found for applied scientist, research engineer, MLOps, inference engineer, edge AI or CV/perception as separate categories in 2025–2026.
- No data on the ratio of PhD-required to non-PhD ML postings.

## 3. New-grad and entry-level realities

### Takeaway
Entry-level tech hiring is sharply down and the evidence is solid. At Big Tech, new grads were 7% of hires in 2024: -25% vs 2023 and more than -50% vs 2019 (SignalFire, May 2025). Stanford payroll research finds a 13% relative employment decline for 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations such as software development (Aug 2025). Layoffs continued in 2026: more than 108k tech layoffs by early May 2026 (layoffs.fyi), and Q1 2026 was the worst quarter since early 2023. Entry-level pay barely moved (Levels.fyi entry median $155K, +1.6% in 2025).

### Cited Findings
**SignalFire State of Talent Report (20 May 2025; data covers 2023–2024)**
- [DATA] Big Tech: new grads were 7% of hires. New-grad hires were down 25% from 2023 and more than 50% from 2019. Startups: new grads under 6% of hires, -11% vs 2023 and more than -30% vs 2019. The share of new grads landing roles at the "Magnificent Seven" has more than halved since 2022. — [SignalFire 2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)
- [DATA, cited] Unemployment for new college grads rose 30% from its Sep 2022 low, vs about 18% for all workers (NY Fed, as cited by SignalFire). Series A startups are 20% smaller than in 2020 (Carta). — [SignalFire 2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)
- [SURVEY] 37% of managers say they would rather use AI than hire a Gen Z employee. 55% of employers say Gen Z struggles with teamwork. — [SignalFire 2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)
- [DATA] Hiring did recover in 2024 for mid- and senior-level roles. The pullback is concentrated at entry level. — [SignalFire 2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)
- [DATA] AI-lab 2-year retention: Anthropic 80%, DeepMind 78%, OpenAI 67%, Meta 64%. Engineers were 8x more likely to move OpenAI to Anthropic than the reverse, and about 11:1 from DeepMind. Based on LinkedIn self-reports. — [SignalFire 2025](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2025)

**Stanford Digital Economy Lab, "Canaries in the Coal Mine?" (Brynjolfsson, Chandar, Chen; Aug 2025)**
- [DATA] ADP payroll data: workers aged 22–25 in the most AI-exposed occupations saw a 13% relative employment decline after controlling for firm shocks. Older workers in the same occupations were flat or growing. Software development and customer support are named as examples. The declines concentrate in occupations where AI automates rather than augments. — [Stanford DEL](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/canaries-in-the-coal-mine-six-facts-about-the-recent-employment-effects-of-artificial-intelligence/); [ADP Research summary](https://www.adpresearch.com/?p=7671)
- [SECONDARY] Employment of 22–25-year-olds in high-exposure jobs fell 6% from late 2022 to Jul 2025. — [ADP Research](https://www.adpresearch.com/?p=7671). The authors call this early evidence, not proof.

**Layoffs**
- [DATA via press] Layoffs.fyi: more than 108,000 tech employees laid off across 137 companies by early May 2026. Q1 2026 had 81,700 tech layoffs (Statista, using layoffs.fyi), the highest quarter since early 2023. Cisco, Meta, Oracle and Block explicitly tied cuts to AI adoption. — [KRON4 (May 2026)](https://www.kron4.com/news/technology-ai/number-of-tech-layoffs-in-2026-already-tops-100k-report/amp/)
- [CONFLICTING] 2025 full year: 122,606 across 278 companies (layoffs.fyi, as widely cited) vs 205,773 across 338 events (Value Add VC). 2026 YTD around Aug: 125,759 (264 companies) vs 205,832 (322 events). The trackers clearly count differently. — [Value Add VC](https://valueaddvc.com/pulse/tech-layoffs-2026-surpass-2025-total); [Voronoi](https://www.voronoiapp.com/technology/Tech-Layoff-Wave-Has-Already-Hit-100000-Jobs-This-Year-8211)
- [SECONDARY] Other trackers: Crunchbase about 127,000 US tech layoffs in 2025. TrueUp: 245,953 workers in 783 events globally in 2025, and 95,878 in 249 events by early May 2026 (faster daily pace than 2025). Challenger, Gray & Christmas: AI cited for 54,836 announced US cuts in 2025 (about 5% of all). — [Pin.com](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)
- [SECONDARY, unverified] One secondary source says the largest 2026 cut was Oracle at about 30,000 and that 54% of 2026 layoff events cite AI. — search summary; treat with caution.

### Inferences
- New-grad ML candidates face a double squeeze: overall entry-level tech hiring is down by half or more vs 2019, and ML postings skew toward experienced and specialist roles. LinkedIn's growth data reflect people *moving into* AI-engineer roles, many of them experienced engineers, not entry-level openings.
- Mid/senior demand recovered in 2024–2025 while junior demand did not. That suggests employers are using AI tools plus experienced hires in place of junior pipelines (consistent with the Stanford DEL paper, though causality is not established).

### Gaps
- No reliable count of new-grad ML/CV openings vs PhD-required research roles. I found no public dataset that splits ML postings by degree requirement.
- Burning Glass Institute entry-level reports were not retrieved.
- No 2026 SignalFire State of Talent primary report was verified, so it is unclear whether one was published.
- No live layoffs.fyi total for October 2026; the latest firm figures are May–Aug 2026 and conflict.

## 4. Compensation

### Takeaway
Overall SWE pay was nearly flat in 2025: the Levels.fyi US median rose about 3.5%, and entry-level was $155K (+1.6%). AI/ML carries a premium that grows with seniority: about +6% at entry rising to about +19% at staff (Levels.fyi Q3 2025, via secondary). The UK AI-skills wage premium was 34% (PwC). Elite AI-lab and Meta superintelligence offers in 2025 reached $10M+/yr and up to $300M over 4 years for a few dozen researchers. These are outliers with no bearing on new-grad pay.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA] Levels.fyi 2025 End of Year report, US median total comp 2024 to 2025: entry-level engineer $152K to $155K (+1.64%); software engineer $222K to $226K (+1.8%); senior $300K to $312K (+4.2%); staff $425K to $457K (+7.52%); principal $590K to $551K (-6.58%). US SWE median TC growth +3.49%. "Research" roles had the highest pay growth at +15.38%. Levels.fyi calls AI/ML "one of the largest and highest-paid SWE tracks in 2025" but the page gives no ML median. — [Levels.fyi 2025 report](https://levels.fyi/2025)
- [SECONDARY] Levels.fyi Q3 2025 AI-engineer premium over non-AI peers at the same level: entry +6.2%, mid +11.9%, senior +14.2%, staff +18.7% (up from 15.8% a year earlier). AI-focused SWEs average $245K. — [Pin.com citing Levels.fyi](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/). Not verified on levels.fyi directly.
- [SURVEY] Robert Half 2026: SWE salary range $109,250–$175,500 vs AI/ML engineer $134,000–$193,250 (midpoint $170,750). Base salary only, broad market rather than Big Tech. — [Pin.com](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)
- [DATA] BLS OES May 2024 (older data): software developer median $133,080. — [Pin.com](https://www.pin.com/blog/tech-job-market-report/)
- [DATA, aggregator] CV engineer pay estimates vary by source: ZipRecruiter about $121.5K average (Jun 2026), Glassdoor $167K median (Jul 2026), Indeed $151.7K average (Aug 2026). Each uses a different method. — [search summary; ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Computer-Vision-Engineer)
- [SURVEY] Underdog.io 2025 survey: ML engineer median about $157K (startup-focused). — [Underdog.io](https://underdog.io/blog/2025-tech-salary-guide)
- [DATA] UK AI-skill wage premium 34.2% in 2025 (up from 11% in 2024). — [PwC UK 2026](https://www.pwc.co.uk/press-room/press-releases/research-commentary/2026/ai-jobs-barometer-2026.html)
- [PRESS, anonymous sources] Meta superintelligence lab (Jun–Jul 2025): offers up to $300M over 4 years with more than $100M in year one (Wired, via The Decoder). At least $10M/yr offers, targeting about 50–100 people (The Register). Ruoming Pang's Apple-to-Meta package was reportedly more than $200M, and Apple raised pay for about 100 engineers in response. — [The Register (13 Jun 2025)](https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/13/meta_offers_10m_ai_researcher/); [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/meta-reportedly-offers-top-openai-researchers-up-to-300-million-over-four-years); [Fortune (11 Jul 2025)](https://fortune.com/2025/07/11/how-much-ai-salary-meta-zuckerberg-200-million-compensation)
- [SECONDARY] Typical Meta pay (Q1 2025, Levels.fyi-style data): AI research scientist base about $179–232K; ML engineer total about $165–440K. — [Mezha](https://mezha.media/en/news/skilki-zaroblyayut-shi-inzheneri-ta-doslidniki-v-meta-marka-cukerberga-303156)

### Inferences
- For new grads, the ML premium is modest (single digits per Levels.fyi Q3 2025). The premium compounds with seniority and specialization, and the big money goes to proven researchers.
- Research-track pay growth (+15%) far outpacing SWE (+3.5%) fits a talent war at the top, not broad wage inflation.

### Gaps
- No verified 2025–2026 new-grad offer figures specific to OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind, Waymo, Tesla, Zoox, Wayve, Aurora, Figure, NVIDIA or Anduril. Levels.fyi company/level pages would need to be checked one by one.
- Levels.fyi's own ML-engineer-vs-SWE median for 2025 was not on the report page.
- No European ML/CV comp benchmarks (London, Zurich, Munich, Paris) were retrieved.

## 5. Which sectors and companies hire CV engineers most (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
Quantitative CV-specific posting volumes by sector or company are essentially unavailable publicly. The evidence is qualitative and points to robotics/humanoids (Tesla Optimus about 110 roles, late 2025), autonomous driving, defense and medical imaging as the main CV/perception employers. Humanoid deployment claims are often inflated: Tesla has never published Optimus production counts. I found no data for Meta Reality Labs, Apple, NVIDIA/Qualcomm, Anduril/Shield AI, Wayve or medical-imaging hiring volumes in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA] Tesla Optimus: about 110 open roles in late 2025. Production targets were a v3 prototype in Feb–Mar 2026 and a hoped-for 1M-unit line by end-2026. — [RoboHorizon (Dec 2025)](https://robohorizon.com/en-us/news/2025/12/tesla-optimus-hiring-spree-hits-110-jobs/); [Teslarati (Mar 2026)](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-optimus-job-listings/)
- [PRESS/analysis] Jul 2026: Tesla has never published an Optimus production count, and credible reporting puts cumulative builds in the low hundreds. Figure and Agility have the strongest verified deployment records. Widely cited "50,000 Optimus / 10,000 Figure" numbers do not come from the companies. — [Technology.org (18 Jul 2026)](https://www.technology.org/2026/07/18/humanoid-robots-in-2026-what-is-actually-deployed/); [humanoid.guide](https://humanoid.guide/humanoid-deployments-in-2026-favor-figure-and-agility/)
