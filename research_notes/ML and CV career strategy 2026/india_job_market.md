# Indian Job Market for ML / DL / CV Roles, 2025–2026 (as of Oct 2026), for a 4th-year ECE/EEE Undergraduate

Research date: 17 Oct 2026. About 15 searches and fetches. Many salary and placement figures come from secondary or SEO career blogs, and each one is flagged where that is the case. Naukri's own blog returned HTTP 403, so Naukri figures come from search snippets of naukri.com pages and from secondary coverage.

## 1. What do NASSCOM/Zinnov, Naukri, LinkedIn, Quess, TeamLease and similar reports say about AI/ML hiring and GCC growth in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
GCCs are the main growth engine for AI/ML hiring in India. Nasscom-Zinnov 2026 counts 2,117 GCCs and about 2.36M employees in FY26. AI/Data is their fastest-growing function (+38% YoY), and about 2 in 3 new GCC roles in 2026 ask for AI or data skills. Naukri shows AI/ML postings growing far faster than overall white-collar hiring: +45% over FY26, slowing to +22% YoY in May 2026. Bengaluru holds roughly 25–30% of AI and GCC hiring, with Hyderabad the fastest-growing Tier-1 city. Every staffing firm reports a large AI skills gap, which favours candidates who can show deployed work. The gap is not a flood of easy fresher openings.

### Cited Findings
**GCC landscape (Nasscom–Zinnov "India GCC Landscape 2026", PDF dated May 2026, data as of Mar 2026/FY26)**
- 2,117 GCCs operating 3,728 units, about 2.36M employees, about US$98.4B revenue in FY26. That is 32% growth since FY2021. Composition: 506 Forbes Global 2000 companies, 583 mid-market GCCs, 504 PE-backed centres. — [Zinnov/Nasscom report page](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/); [PDF](https://media.zinnov.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report.pdf); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/indias-gcc-industry-records-984-billion-revenue-in-fy26-report-says)
- AI, Data Science and Analytics is the fastest-growing GCC function at +38% YoY. Nearly half of GCCs set up since FY2021 had AI as a core focus from the start. — [Swarajya, 2 Jul 2026](https://swarajyamag.com/economy/ai-data-skills-now-drive-two-in-three-new-gcc-jobs-in-india-as-hiring-rises-11-per-cent); [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/others/from-delivery-engines-to-ai-nerve-centers-indias-gccs-take-the-global-lead/)

Some coverage presents 250k as a 2026 projection ("nearly double") and some as a current count, so the figure is uncertain. — [Zinnov report page](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/) (via search summary; not verified in the PDF text)

**GCC hiring, H1 2026 (Swarajya, 2 Jul 2026, citing Business Today; the underlying data source is not named)**
- GCCs hired 227,991 people in Jan–Jun 2026, up 11% YoY, while overall white-collar hiring fell 9% YoY in June 2026. — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/economy/ai-data-skills-now-drive-two-in-three-new-gcc-jobs-in-india-as-hiring-rises-11-per-cent)

Tech/Software plus BFSI GCCs account for 56% of it. — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/economy/ai-data-skills-now-drive-two-in-three-new-gcc-jobs-in-india-as-hiring-rises-11-per-cent)

Attrition is 8–12% in Tier-2 cities against 18–22% in Tier-1. — [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/economy/ai-data-skills-now-drive-two-in-three-new-gcc-jobs-in-india-as-hiring-rises-11-per-cent)

**Skills-gap reports (staffing firms)**

Senior AI roles pay up to ₹60 LPA. TeamLease calls some of these figures directional. — [YourStory, Sep 2026](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/talent-gap-genai-cloud-india-stands-53-60-teamlease-digital-report); [Gulte](https://www.gulte.com/trends/434492/10-lakh-ai-jobs-but-only-16-of-it-workers-have-ai-skills)
- Quess Corp: India's AI workforce is about 9.2 lakh, of whom only about 2.57 lakh work in advanced LLM/ML areas, while about 70% of demand targets those areas. Quess also reports about 3.5 lakh AI job openings within 90 days. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/in-depth/indias-ai-talent-paradox-companies-ready-to-pay-more-but-few-skilled-hands-available); [HRKatha](https://www.hrkatha.com/news/india-sees-3-5-lakh-ai-job-openings-in-90-days-report/)
- Quess GCC report (Feb 2026, which counted about 1,900 GCCs): 43% talent gap in AI/data/analytics, and 18–43% supply gaps across high-demand domains. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/indias-1900-gccs-face-18-43-talent-shortfall-in-key-tech-roles-quess-corp-90866.htm)

### Inferences
- AI/ML postings growth is slowing, from +49% in Feb 2026 to +22% in May 2026, but it is still several times overall white-collar growth (about 0–12%). The fastest growth is at the high-salary end (₹30–50 LPA+), so demand leans toward experienced or specialist talent rather than freshers.
- With 56% of GCC hiring at 4–10 years of experience, freshers compete for a smaller slice, roughly 30% "early career", which also includes 1–3 year profiles.
- The skills-gap reports measure skill shortfalls, not vacant fresher seats. They do not mean freshers will find it easy to get hired.
- Bengaluru (about 25–30%) and Hyderabad (about 12.5%, growing fastest) should be the primary geographic targets. Pune, Delhi-NCR and Chennai come next for automotive/embedded CV.

### Gaps
- No Deloitte or Indeed India AI hiring report for 2025–26 turned up in these searches.
- The Nasscom-Zinnov PDF was not read directly. The city split of GCCs and the exact AI/ML headcount wording in the PDF are unverified.
- No source isolates computer vision (as opposed to ML or GenAI generally) in posting counts.
- No Naukri JobSpeak releases after May 2026 (Jun–Sep 2026) were found.

## 2. Which employers hire ML/CV freshers, and what roles do they post?

### Takeaway
Most entry-level ML/CV routes for a BTech ECE/EEE graduate are not titled "ML Engineer – fresher". They come through campus SWE/ASIC/embedded tracks at semiconductor and automotive GCCs, research fellow and pre-doc programmes at labs, internships that convert to full-time at startups, and specialised tracks at IT services firms. Many posted CV/DL roles at NVIDIA and Qualcomm in India are senior. Only limited verified new-grad postings were found.

### Cited Findings
- **NVIDIA India:** a "Senior Software Engineer – Deep Learning and Computer Vision" role (Broadcast/Maxine video and 3D neural networks) is listed for Pune and Bengaluru at senior level. NVIDIA's "Developer Technology Engineer, AI – New College Grad 2026" role found in the search was in Santa Clara (posted 3 Mar 2026), and no India equivalent was found. — [BuiltIn Bengaluru](https://builtinbengaluru.in/job/senior-software-engineer-deep-learning-and-computer-vision/7613691); [InferenceJobs](https://inferencejobs.com/companies/nvidia/jobs/developer-technology-engineer-ai-new-college-grad-2026)

Qualcomm India's entry route appears as "Associate Engineer – SW" postings, seen on a job-alert Telegram channel (unverified). — [ABVen/Nuvia listing](https://jobs.abven.com/companies/nuvia/jobs/64826238-computer-vision-ai-engineer-up-to-senior-level); [Bayt](https://www.bayt.com/en/india/jobs/lead-engineer-senior-computer-vision-74881221/); [Telegram](https://t.me/s/codewithrup?after=2662)

Edge-CV startups such as Netradyne and Ati Motors value embedded skills alongside DL. This is inferred from posting patterns. No 2026 new-grad CV posting from these firms was verified.
- Research Fellow and pre-doc programmes (MSR India, GDM India) are among the few formal BTech-to-research routes. They need recommendation letters and research output, so applying requires lead time. MSR's cycle opened around January.

## 3. Compensation bands for freshers and 0–3 years in ML/CV roles

Top-campus medians are about ₹22–28 LPA (IIT/BITS, secondary sources). Big-tech new-grad offers sit above that, but levels.fyi data could not be retrieved.

## 4. Campus placements vs off-campus in 2025–2026; internships and referrals

### Takeaway
Top campuses had AI/ML-heavy 2025–26 seasons, with medians flat to slightly up per secondary sources. Disclosure has become restrictive, so the true picture is murky. Off-campus fresher hiring is growing modestly: Naukri freshers were +7–17% YoY in Feb–May 2026. Recruiters look for proof of real project work, and PPOs from internships make up a large share of offers.

### Cited Findings
- IIT Delhi 2025–26 Phase 1: 1,275+ offers, 1,140+ students placed, 300+ PPOs. Status ongoing in 2026. — [CollegeDost](https://collegedost.in/blog/engineering-colleges/iit-delhi-placements/); [Vedantu](https://www.vedantu.com/jee-advanced/iit-delhi-highest-package-and-placement)

No package disclosure. — [Careers360 News](https://news.careers360.com/iit-roorkee-placements-2025-8-international-428-domestic-job-offers-btech-engineering-day-1-ai-data-analytics-in-focus/amp)

## 5. India initiatives: IndiaAI Mission, compute, semiconductor push (ISM), and implications for ML/CV/edge-AI jobs

### Takeaway
IndiaAI has built a subsidised GPU pool of about 34–38k GPUs, with about 20k more announced in Feb 2026, at about ₹65/GPU-hour. That supports startups and foundation-model builders (Sarvam and others) who hire ML engineers. ISM 2.0 funds fabs, OSAT and design (DLI). Its direct job counts are modest, and India's main strength remains chip design, which matches ECE graduates' edge-AI and hardware-ML skills.

### Inferences
- IndiaAI compute mainly increases demand at funded foundation-model and AI startups (LLM, speech, Indic language) rather than CV directly. ECE graduates benefit most where these firms need inference, on-device or edge optimisation (e.g., Sarvam's on-device AI intern role).
- ISM's fab and OSAT jobs are mostly process, equipment and manufacturing roles, not ML. The relevant overlap for ML/CV is chip design and edge-AI accelerator work at design GCCs and DLI startups, where India's strength is design. The DLI target of only 200 additional design professionals in 2026–27 is small. The larger hiring volume remains at multinational design centres.
